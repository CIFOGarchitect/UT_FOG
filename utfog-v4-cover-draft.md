# Utah Interoperable Communications Field Operations Guide

**Version 4.0 — January 2027**

**Authority:** Statewide Interoperability Coordinator, under the
direction of the Statewide Interoperability Executive Committee.

**Published by:** Statewide Interoperability Coordinator, Utah Division
of Emergency Management, Department of Public Safety (DPS/DEM).

**Distribution:** Controlled — Controlled Unclassified Information
(CUI). Distribution limited to authorized users.

**Supersession:** This version supersedes Version 3.0 (November 2022).

**Licensing note (carried forward):** Nothing in this guide shall be
construed to serve as authorization for any agency or individual to
operate equipment on radio channels without first obtaining proper
authorization or licensing by the Federal Communications Commission
(FCC).
